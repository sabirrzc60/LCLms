🎯 SEC Command Verbs (How Marks Are Awarded)

State / List / Identify: Give a concise, direct fact or term. No long essay needed (usually 2m or 3m per point).
Explain / Account for: Give the scientific mechanism ("how" or "why"). State the point AND link it to cause-and-effect (e.g., "Lime raises pH, which increases earthworm & bacterial activity, improving nutrient availability").
Evaluate / Discuss: Weigh up both advantages and limitations using agricultural evidence, then give a reasoned judgement.
Design an Investigation: Always include: (1) Hypothesis, (2) Independent & Dependent variables, (3) Control & fixed variables, (4) Replication/sample size, (5) Data recorded & safety.
