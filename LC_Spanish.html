Official 400-Mark Exam Blueprint

How the State Examinations Commission (SEC) awards every mark at Higher Level.

Total: 400 Marks (H1 = 360–400m / 90%+)
Component 1 • Easter 100 Marks (25%)

Oral Examination (15 Mins)

  • • General Conversation (~10 mins): Family, school, pastimes, technology, past holidays, future plans & current affairs.
  • • Roleplay (~5 mins): 1 of 5 prescribed scenarios + examiner's follow-up Open Question.
Component 2 • June (40 Mins) 80 Marks (20%)

Aural / Listening Test

  • • 7 Audio Sections: Dialogues, announcements, weather report, and 3 journalistic news items.
  • • Answers in English: Each piece is played 3 times (whole, segments, whole). Precision wins full marks.
Component 3 • June (2h 30m) 220 Marks (55%)

Written Paper (Sections A, B & C)

  • • Section A (70m): Prescriptive & journalistic reading comprehensions.
  • • Section B (100m): Long reading text (60m) + Opinion Piece in Spanish (40m).
  • • Section C (50m): Dialogue/Letter (30m) + Diary Entry/Note (20m).
