Specification Strands, Themes & SEC Assessment Structure

Leaving Certificate Biology is assessed across two components totaling 500 marks: a 60% Written Examination (300 marks, 2 hours 30 minutes) and a 40% Biology in Practice Investigation (200 marks). Reaching an H1 (90%+) requires command of both strands and precise scientific language.

Written Examination • 60% (300 Marks)

2 Hours 30 Minutes • Question 1 + 5 of 6

  • Question 1 (50 marks — Compulsory): Multi-part question drawing across diverse syllabus topics—no topic can be safely ignored.
  • Questions 2–7 (50 marks each — Choose any 5): Six structured/extended questions spanning the unifying strand, contextual strands, and cross-cutting themes.
  • Total answered: 6 questions × 50 marks = 300 marks (~25 minutes per question at even pace).
  • Units & working: Show full mathematical working and include units wherever relevant.
Biology in Practice Investigation • 40% (200 Marks)

SEC Common Brief • Research, Primary Data & Report

  • Common brief & timeline: Respond to the SEC investigation brief in 5th/6th year (~20 hours guided work, submitted ahead of the written exam).
  • Core stages: Secondary source research, testable hypothesis, designed primary experiment with controls/replicates, data analysis & evaluation, and investigation log/report.
  • H1 edge: Bank strong marks here before June by linking biological mechanisms directly to quantitative evidence and realistic limitations.

Unifying Strand, Contextual Strands & Cross-Cutting Themes

Unifying Strand: Nature of Science

Scientific inquiry, fair testing, variables, reliability/validity, peer evidence, ethical/societal implications, and how biological models evolve.

Three Contextual Strands

1. Organisation of Life • 2. Structures & Processes of Life • 3. Interactions of Life (ecology, genetics, evolution, physiology).

Cross-Cutting Themes

Health, Sustainability, and Technology woven into scenarios, data interpretation, and extended H1 responses.

H1 Marking Scheme Tactics

1. Name exact structures & molecules: Write thylakoid membrane / stroma, mitochondrial matrix / inner membrane, glomerulus / proximal convoluted tubule, mRNA / tRNA / codon / anticodon rather than vague substitutes.   2. Match the command word: Outline = concise key stages; Compare = explicit similarity or difference stating both items; Justify / Evaluate = claim + biological reasoning + evidence/limitation.   3. Never leave surplus conflicting answers when a question asks for a specific number or single term.

Zero-to-Hero Levels
Lab 1 • Ecology & Biodiversity

Simpson’s Diversity Index (Ds) & Quadrat Calculator

Formula: D_s = 1 - [ Σ n(n - 1) / N(N - 1) ], where n is the number of individuals of a species and N is total individuals across all species in the sample. Higher D_s (closer to 1) indicates greater species diversity.

Lab 2 • Mendelian Genetics & Test Crosses

Monohybrid & Test-Cross Ratio Explorer

Explore expected gamete formation, Punnett grid outcomes, genotypic and phenotypic ratios, and how a test cross distinguishes homozygous dominant (AA) from heterozygous (Aa) individuals.

Lab 3 • Physiology & Bioenergetics

Enzyme Activity & Limiting Factor Simulator

Adjust temperature (°C) and pH to see how kinetic collisions and active-site tertiary structure shape reaction rate (relative % of Vmax).
